## Ingredients
- Milk
    - 1.5 cups
- Butter
    - 4 tablespoons
- Light brown sugar
    - 1/3 Cup
- Ground cinnamon
    - 1 1/2 teaspoons
- Salt
    - 1 1/2 teaspoons
- Bread flour
    - 4 cups
- Yeast
    - 1 1/2 teaspoons
- Raisins
    - 1 cup

## Directions
1. Pour milk into bread bucket
1. Add other ingredients (**except** raisins) to bucket
    - Add flour and then yeast last
1. Put bucket into bread machine
1. Put raisins into machine's fruit box and attach to top of machine.
    - Entire 1 cup doesn't fit.
    - **Do not pack tightly** because raisins won't fall out when the box opens
1. Menu option **#1 (Soft Bread)**
    - Light color
    - Largest loaf size
1. Start machine
1. Let cool in bucket for a bit
1. Use spatula to separate bread from bucket sides
1. Cool on wire rack for at least an hour

## Wait Times
- Optional **1-2 hours** before starting for butter to soften

## Things to Try
- Try Golden Raisins
- Try up to 3 teaspoons of cinnamon
