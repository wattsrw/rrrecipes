## Ingredients
- boneless leg of lamb
- garlic
    - 15 cloves
- parsley
    - 1/4 cup
- rosemary
    - 5 tablespoons 
- salt
    - 1 teaspoon per pound of meat
- black pepper
- olive oil
    - 2 tablespoons
- water
    - 1 1/2 cups

## Directions
1. Mince 5 cloves of garlic and parsley
1. Roughly slice remainder garlic
1. Chop rosemary
1. Preheat the oven to **450** degrees.
1. Trim the lamb
    - Remove the outer membrane: Peel back and slice away the tough outer layer of tissue, known as the fell, if you want a milder flavor and better seasoning penetration.
    - Trim heavy fat: Cut away thick layers of hard fat down to an even layer about 1/4-inch thick so the meat cooks evenly.
    - Remove silver skin and glands: Peel off any shiny, rubbery silver skin and locate the small triangular fat pocket hiding the lymph gland inside the seams, which can impart a bitter or overly gamey taste.
1. Open the lamb up (inside facing up) and drizzle a tablespoon of olive oil on the lamb’s interior.  Season with half of the salt and pepper and all of the garlic paste.  Note: Use 3/4 to 1 teaspoon of kosher salt per pound of meat for the whole roast.
1. Sprinkle all of the parsley and half of the rosemary into the inside of the lamb
1. Lay pieces of butcher’s twine out and tie the lamb tightly into a cylinder. Tie regular knots about 2 inches apart.
1. Cut slits into all of the exterior areas of the lamb.  Make each slit about 2 inches apart and the depth of the garlic clove pieces.
1. Push the garlic pieces into the slits.
1. Coat the lamb with the remaining olive oil.  Use hands to spread it all over.  Season the lamb’s exterior with the remaining salt, pepper, and rosemary.
1. Place the seasoned lamb on a wire rack in a roasting pan. Pour 1 1/2 cups of water into the roasting pan making sure to not pour too high and hit the lamb’s underside.
1. Roast the lamb for 15 minutes then turn the heat down to **350** degrees.
1. Continue roasting the lamb about **20 minutes per pound**.
1. Remove the lamb from the oven and tent with foil for 10-15 minutes before carving.
